Rules

  1. RULE 01

    The father kills a daughter if the mother is not there. Never leave the father with either daughter on a bank without the mother.

  2. RULE 02

    The mother kills a son if the father is not there. Never leave the mother with either son on a bank without the father.

  3. RULE 03

    The dog kills the whole family if the servant is not there. Never leave the dog with any family member without the servant. The dog and the servant alone are fine.

  4. RULE 04

    The boat seats two. Only the father, the mother and the servant can row it, and one of them must be aboard on every trip — going across and coming back.

How to play

  1. Press Start. The clock starts the moment you do. Nobody can be moved before that.

  2. Tap a character to put them in the boat. Only people standing on the bank where the boat is can board. Tap again to take them back out. The boat seats two.

  3. Press Cross. The boat will not move unless someone with a vermilion border — the father, the mother or the servant — is aboard. Anyone in the boat counts as being off the bank, and both banks are checked the moment the boat departs.

  4. Break a rule and you get an incident. Undo and Restart are right there in the dialog. The clock keeps running through incidents, so feel free to experiment.

  5. Get all eight across to win. You will see your move count and your time. Fewest moves and fastest time are recorded separately, so you can chase either one.

About this puzzle

This is a classic river-crossing puzzle, known in English as the "jealous husbands" problem and in Japanese as 猛獣家族の川渡り. It belongs to the same family as the wolf, goat and cabbage riddle, and as "missionaries and cannibals".

What makes it harder is the scale. There are eight characters rather than three, and only the father, the mother and the servant can row. Every trip forces you to think about two things at once: who is left behind, and who arrives on the other side.

Seventeen is the minimum

The puzzle can be solved in 17 crossings, and that is the theoretical minimum. There are only 512 states in total — 28 = 256 arrangements of the eight characters, times two positions for the boat — and a breadth-first search over all of them confirms that no solution of 16 or fewer exists.

A first attempt usually lands somewhere between 25 and 40 crossings. If you reach 17, there is nothing left to shave off.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fewest number of crossings?

Seventeen. A breadth-first search over all 512 states confirms that no solution of 16 or fewer exists.

Who can row the boat?

Only the father, the mother and the servant. One of them must be aboard on every trip, in both directions, so the children and the dog can never move the boat on their own.

Is it safe to leave the dog alone with the servant?

Yes. The dog only attacks the six family members, and the servant is not one of them. The dog on its own is safe too.

Does someone sitting in the boat still count as being on the bank?

No. Once someone boards, they count as having left the bank, and both banks are checked for safety the moment you press Cross.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. There is no install and no sign-up — it runs in the browser. Tap a character to put them in the boat, then press Cross.
